# Railhq (from getrailhq.com and each product's pricing page)

Railhq is a family of lean SaaS tools for indie founders and small startups. One Railhq Pass ($29/month, founding price) includes all of them with one login (accounts.getrailhq.com).
Products in the Pass:
- Kickrail: affiliate and referral tracking for Stripe, from first click to every recurring invoice. Replaces Rewardful ($99/mo Growth plan).
- Mailrail: email campaigns, automations and signup forms, with a Brevo-compatible API so switching is a config change. Replaces Brevo ($99/mo).
- Pathrail: privacy-friendly web analytics with revenue by channel, AI crawler visits and bot filtering. Replaces Plausible ($99/mo Business plan).
- Demorail: interactive product demos captured from your real app, with AI-written steps. Replaces Storylane ($50/mo Starter).
- Seorail: keyword rankings, site audits and AI visibility tracking. An alternative to Semrush and Ahrefs.
Kickrail is open source on GitHub. Together the replaced tools cost about $347/month; the Pass is $29.

Brand colours: Kickrail #0f5e4b, Mailrail #4f46e5, Pathrail #0e6b62, Demorail #3730a3, Seorail #116d52.
