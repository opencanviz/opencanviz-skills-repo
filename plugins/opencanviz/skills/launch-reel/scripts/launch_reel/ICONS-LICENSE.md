# Icons in icons.json

`icons.json` holds icon paths taken from the Iconify API (https://iconify.design), flattened to
path data and fill colours. They come from these sets:

- `ph:` Phosphor Icons, https://github.com/phosphor-icons/core, MIT License,
  Copyright (c) 2023 Phosphor Icons
- `logos:` SVG Logos by Gil Barbara, https://github.com/gilbarbara/logos, CC0 1.0
- `simple-icons:` Simple Icons, https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons, CC0 1.0

The brand marks in `logos:` and `simple-icons:` are trademarks of their owners. Use them only
to say that a product works with that tool, and only when it does.
